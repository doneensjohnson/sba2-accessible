# design-task
What accessibility challenges did you face, and how did you address them?
Identifying the roles and including necessary roles in the hmtl was difficult, because it is unclear across various resources of the proper way to format the code. Attempting to capture the various accessibility areas and formatting was a challenge. There are areas that I would like to simply add colors and fancy fonts, but that limits the accessibility, so I had to be mindful. I was not able to get the media queries to cooperate to size according to different screens.

How did you ensure that your design was responsive and accessible to all users?
I tried to keep the formatting simple and enlarged the font. I used Lighthouse to score my accessibility and it returned a 86 due to missing aria labels.

What tools or resources did you find most helpful during this project?
The Ba-Sing-Se contact form was very helpful! Thank you for that resource! Being routed to the blank page made me inquire about how I could send my submission to another link and I was successful in making that happen.
